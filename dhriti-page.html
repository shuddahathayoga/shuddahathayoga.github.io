The yoga of the intellect. Seeing life clearly, as it is.

Questions

Answered.

Do I need any prior yoga experience?

No. Prior experience of yoga is not needed. Every practice is taught live, step by step, by certified Sadhguru Gurukulam Hatha Yoga teachers, with corrections towards the ideal posture.

What will I learn in the 21 days?
  • Simple yet powerful Isha Hatha Yoga practices
  • Guided meditations for peace, health and wellbeing
  • A simple Kriya Yoga practice
  • The yogic perspective on food, with tips suggested by Sadhguru
  • Daily guided sadhana, with Q&A support for your doubts
When does it start, and how long is each session?

The batch begins on Monday, 9 November 2026 and runs live for 21 days. Each session is 90 minutes, and you choose one of five daily timings: 3:30 am, 6:00 am, 8:00 am, 4:00 pm or 6:00 pm IST.

Outside India? Pick your country under "Show times for" and the timings change to your local time.

Do I have to attend every session?

Yes. Attendance in all sessions is mandatory, so please choose the batch timing you can keep every day for all 21 days.

How do I join the live sessions?

The program is fully online. After you register and pay, we add you to the program's WhatsApp group and send you the session link before the batch begins.

Can I join from outside India?

Yes. Choose your country to see the batch times in your time zone, then pay the ₹999 fee with an international Visa or Mastercard card. Your bank converts it to your currency.

How much does it cost, and how do I pay?

The fee is ₹999 for the full 21 days. You can pay securely through Razorpay by UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit or credit card, netbanking or wallet. Your place is confirmed on screen as soon as the payment goes through.

I have a health condition. Can I still join?

Please mention any health condition in the registration form. This helps your teacher adapt the practices safely for you. If you are unsure whether the program suits you, call or WhatsApp us before registering.

Who are the teachers?

Your sessions are led by certified Sadhguru Gurukulam Hatha Yoga teachers. Shudda Hathayoga has taught Classical Hatha Yoga since 2017, with centres in Hyderabad, Nellore, Chittoor, Hosur and Kochi.

I had a problem with payment. What should I do?

Don't worry. If money was deducted but the payment did not complete, it is refunded to your account automatically, usually within 5 to 7 days. For any help, call or WhatsApp us on 9391020729.
