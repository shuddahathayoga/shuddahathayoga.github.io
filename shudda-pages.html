Do I need to know yoga to join?+

No. Most members begin with our 21-day Dhriti program, and every Dhriti participant is invited to membership at the closing session. You can also join Prarambha directly.

Where do the in-person sessions happen?+

Sadhana and Samarpana sessions are held in person in Bangalore. Write to us on WhatsApp for the current venue and batch timings.

Can I move up a step later?+

Yes. You can move from Prarambha to Sadhana, or from Sadhana to Samarpana, at any time. We adjust what you have already paid.

What if I miss sessions?+

Regular sessions are flexible. Sessions where a new practice is taught are important, so we give you a catch-up session if you miss one.
