One program, four paths

Head, heart, hands and energy.

Gnana Yoga ज्ञान

The yoga of the intellect. Seeing life clearly, as it is.

Bhakti Yoga भक्ति

The yoga of devotion. Turning emotion into a sweet longing.

Karma Yoga कर्म

The yoga of action, with Hatha Yoga as part of it. Doing what is needed with the body and mind, without entanglement.

Kriya Yoga क्रिया

The yoga of energy. Inner practices that work on your life force.
